Week 1: Greetings

Objectives:

- Students will be able to recognize and understand various greetings.

- Students will practice introducing themselves to others.

- Students will develop the ability to ask questions about others' identities.

Steps of the Lesson:

1. Introduction (5 minutes):

- Welcome the students and introduce the topic of greetings.

- Discuss the importance of greetings in different contexts and cultures.

2. Listening for Details (15 minutes):

- Play an audio recording containing different greetings.

- Instruct students to listen carefully and note down the greetings they hear.

3. Speaking about Yourself (20 minutes):

- Model introducing yourself to the class, including name, age, nationality, and a hobby.

- Pair students and have them take turns introducing themselves to each other.

- Encourage students to ask follow-up questions to learn more about their classmates.

4. Asking Questions about Others (15 minutes):

- Provide a list of questions about personal information (e.g., Where are you from? What's your favorite food?).

- Pair students and have them ask each other questions about their identities.

- Circulate and provide assistance as needed.

Materials:

- Audio recording of greetings

- Handout with questions for speaking activities

Evaluation:

- Participation and engagement during speaking activities.

- Ability to correctly identify and use greetings.

- Effective communication skills demonstrated during introductions and questioning.

Worksheets:

- Handout with questions for speaking activities

Week 2-3: Physical Appearance

Objectives:

- Students will be able to describe physical appearance using appropriate vocabulary.

- Students will practice describing someone's appearance.

- Students will develop their listening skills by identifying the main topic of a conversation.

Steps of the Lesson:

1. Introduction (5 minutes):

- Review the previous lesson on greetings.

- Introduce the topic of physical appearance.

2. Listening for Topic (15 minutes):

- Play an audio recording discussing the importance of physical appearance in society.

- Ask students to listen for the main topic and any key points.

3. Describing Someone (20 minutes):

- Provide pictures of people with different physical features.

- Pair students and have them describe the people in the pictures to each other.

- Encourage the use of descriptive language and vocabulary related to physical appearance.

4. Discussion and Practice (15 minutes):

- Lead a class discussion about the descriptions.

- Encourage students to share their observations and provide feedback to their peers.

- Have students practice describing a classmate's appearance to the rest of the class.

Materials:

- Audio recording discussing physical appearance

- Pictures of people with different physical features

- Whiteboard and markers for class discussion

Evaluation:

- Accuracy and effectiveness of physical descriptions provided by students.

- Participation and engagement during class discussion and activities.

- Listening comprehension demonstrated during the audio activity.

Worksheets:

- Instructions: Describe a person's appearance to your partner. Your partner will fill in the worksheet with the described features.

- Worksheet includes space for students to fill in descriptions of a person's physical appearance.

Week 4-5: Describing Clothes

Objectives:

- Students will be able to describe clothing items using appropriate vocabulary.

- Students will practice making decisions about what to wear for different occasions.

- Students will develop their listening skills by identifying the main idea of a conversation.

Steps of the Lesson:

1. Introduction (5 minutes):

- Review the previous lesson on physical appearance.

- Introduce the topic of describing clothes.

2. Listening for Gist (15 minutes):

- Play an audio recording describing different outfits.

- Ask students to listen for the overall message or main idea of the conversation.

3. Deciding What to Wear (20 minutes):

- Present different scenarios (e.g., going to a party, to the beach) and have students discuss and decide what they would wear.

- Encourage justification for their choices based on the scenario provided.

4. Discussion and Practice (15 minutes):

- Lead a class discussion about the clothing choices made for each scenario.

- Encourage students to explain their reasoning and consider the appropriateness of their choices for different events.

- Have students share their own experiences of deciding what to wear for specific occasions.

Materials:

- Audio recording describing outfits

- Scenario cards for activity

- Whiteboard and markers for class discussion

Evaluation:

- Accuracy and effectiveness of clothing descriptions provided by students.

- Participation and engagement during class discussion and activities.

- Application of vocabulary and decision-making skills demonstrated during the activity.

Worksheets:

- Worksheet: "What to Wear"

- Scenario cards with different events (e.g., party, job interview)

- Space for students to write down their clothing choices and justify them

3 Classroom management

Clear expectations for behavior and participation were established, ensuring students understood the importance of active listening and respectful speaking during class activities. Variety of interactive strategies were employed, such as structured group discussions, pair work and targeted listening exercises, to enhance student engagement and foster communicative competence.

Consistent feedback and positive reinforcement were utilized to motivate students, while differentiated support was provided to address diverse learning needs, promoting an inclusive and effective learning environment.

4 Individual Development Plan

Strategies aimed at developing Students' Listening and Speaking skills:

Listening: Students are performing listening exercises with increasing complexity, such as podcasts, dialogues, and recorded speeches, followed by comprehension questions. Participating in listening activities where students summarize or respond to the ideas presented.

Speaking: I incorporated role-playing, debates and storytelling sessions to encourage students to speak on a variety of topics. Each student was provided with opportunities to present brief speeches or arguments, with a focus on improving coherence, pronunciation, and vocabulary usage. Feedback: Regular, individualized feedback on pronunciation, tone, grammar, and content organization was given to identify areas for improvement and track progress.
